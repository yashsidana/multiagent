# Enterprise Product Pricing, Discounting & Packaging Guide

## 1. Product Catalog Overview
* **Enterprise AI Platform License**: $45,000 per annual production instance. Includes unlimited internal read-only users, standard support, and monthly updates.
* **Cloud Compute Dedicated Cluster**: $12,000 per node-month. High-throughput GPU nodes allocated strictly on dedicated VPC endpoints.
* **Architectural Advisory Sprint**: $25,000 per sprint package (equivalent to 80 senior solution architect hours). Covers schema migration, security audits, and agent deployment.
* **Edge AI Inference Appliance**: $8,500 per hardware unit. On-premise industrial ruggedized server for low-latency inference.

## 2. Customer Tier Discounting Matrix
Sales representatives are authorized to grant discounts subject to strict tier limits:
1. **Enterprise Tier** (Annual spend > $250,000):
   * Up to **10.0%** discretionary discount on Software Licenses and Cloud Compute.
   * Advisory sprints may receive up to 5% bundling discounts when packaged with annual platform commitments.
2. **Mid-Market Tier** (Annual spend $75,000 – $250,000):
   * Maximum **5.0%** discount on software licenses.
   * Discounts on hardware appliances are prohibited without Regional Director sign-off.
3. **SMB Tier** (Annual spend < $75,000):
   * Standard list pricing applies. Discretionary discounting is 0.00%.

## 3. Discontinued & Grandfathered Products
* The **Legacy Data Connector Pack** was retired from active sales on November 1, 2024.
* Upgrades from Legacy Connector to Enterprise AI Platform qualify for a 20% trade-in credit against year 1 licensing fees.
