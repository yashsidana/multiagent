# Corporate Return, Refund & Warranty Policy

## 1. General Principles
Our policy governs all customer returns, subscription cancellations, and dispute resolutions across both software and physical appliance offerings.

## 2. Software Licenses & Digital Subscriptions
* **Non-Refundable Policy**: Licenses for the **Enterprise AI Platform License** and related software modules are strictly non-refundable once the digital license keys have been generated and delivered to the customer portal.
* **Exceptions**: If a critical software defect renders the platform completely inoperable for more than 14 consecutive business days without a workaround from customer engineering, customers may request a prorated credit toward consulting or cloud compute hours upon VP approval.

## 3. Hardware & Edge Appliances
* **Return Window**: Physical hardware (including the **Edge AI Inference Appliance**) carries a 30-day return window starting from the verified delivery carrier timestamp.
* **Condition**: Appliances must be returned in their original packaging, unopened with security tamper seals intact. A 15% restocking fee applies to all non-defective returns.
* **Defective on Arrival (DOA)**: Units received with transit damage or hardware faults must be reported within 48 hours. Upon RMA (Return Merchandise Authorization) issuance, a replacement unit is expedited within 3 business days, or the customer may elect a 100% full refund with all shipping surcharges waived (e.g., the policy invocation applied to Order #106 for Sao Paulo Retailers in Dec 2024).

## 4. Consulting & Advisory Services
* Consulting sprints (such as the Architectural Advisory Sprint) require at least 10 business days advance cancellation prior to scheduled commencement.
* Sprints cancelled with less than 5 days notice forfeit 50% of the milestone deposit.
